## About Me

I'm Bekzat, a Software Engineering student at **Astana IT University**, a **Software Engineer at ESEP**, and an **AI/ML student at Tomorrow School**.

I'm passionate about software development, artificial intelligence, and building practical applications. I enjoy solving algorithmic problems on LeetCode, exploring new technologies, and turning ideas into real-world products.

## Featured Work

### [Steppe — Local AI for Meetings](https://github.com/gojosatorukz/steppe-meeting-intelligence)

I built the React/TypeScript interface for this local multilingual meeting assistant, connected it to the live transcription and Q&A APIs, and prepared the demo.

### [Forte Cash Monitor — Video Review with AI](https://github.com/gojosatorukz/forte-cash-monitor)

I led the Datathon finalist team, selected models, prepared video data, and worked on training and evaluating the detector for cash-room footage.

### [On the Spot — AI English Speaking Coach](https://github.com/gojosatorukz/zimran-business-cup-2026/tree/main/apps/on-the-spot)

I built this workplace English practice app end to end, including voice transcription, practice drills, and AI feedback checked against the user's answers.

### [TVmaze — Reproducible ML Study](https://github.com/gojosatorukz/tvmaze-series-classification)

I developed the technical work for this AITU course project and extended it into a reproducible machine learning study. I collected the TVmaze dataset, compared metadata and synopsis models on identical splits, tuned models with nested cross-validation, and used error analysis and a separate catalogue to test whether audience data improved classification.
